## API

`Sonner` uses the same container option names as Toast.

`defaults` accepts `ToastDefaults`; individual `toast(...)`, semantic commands, and `toast.custom(...)` accept `ToastOptions`. Both share the `classes` slots. `styles` configures only action and cancel buttons. A button’s `action.style` or `cancel.style` takes precedence over its corresponding `styles` entry.

`toast.promise` accepts `ToastPromiseOptions<Data>` and preserves loading, success, error, description, finally, and the returned `unwrap()` method. The success and error results may be ReactNode or a `ToastPromiseMessage` containing `message`; notification options in those results are adapted too. `getHistory()` and `getToasts()` retain the upstream return model and field names. `id` and `toasterId` keep their routing behavior; `useToast()` returns commands for its Provider channel.

## Accessibility

- Preserves underlying semantics, focus management, and keyboard interaction.
- Provide sensible names for triggers, icons, and states.

## Avoid

- Don't remove focus styles or bypass state properties.
- Don’t write down business copy, dimensions, and product status.
